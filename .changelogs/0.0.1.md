* First release.
* Lets mods use assets from other mods' jars without shipping them: the player gets the jar from its official page, and Dep Ensurer keeps it from loading as a mod and serves its assets to the game.
* Missing jars are listed on a screen with links to their pages.
* The CurseForge build only uses jars that are installed. The other builds can also download missing ones from Modrinth, CurseForge or the mirrors a mod lists, and check each file.
